# MouseLens 30-Day Content Calendar

Updated: 2026-07-14

## Strategy

The first 30 days should build a repeatable content engine around clear use cases instead of broad brand awareness. The content should show real before/after value: raw recording vs. MouseLens-style demo, confusing cursor movement vs. cursor emphasis, long recording vs. trimmed export, static product update vs. GIF/MP4 demo.

Primary channels:

- X / Twitter
- LinkedIn
- Product communities and indie-maker communities
- Short video platforms
- Dev/product newsletters or personal blog

Primary content formats:

- 15-30 second demo clips.
- Before/after comparison posts.
- Workflow GIFs.
- Short product-building notes.
- Launch-progress updates for 1.2.

## Weekly Themes

| Week | Theme | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Week 1 | Define the problem | Show why raw screen recordings are hard to follow |
| Week 2 | Show the workflow | Demonstrate smart zoom, cursor emphasis, backgrounds, MP4/GIF export |
| Week 3 | Use-case expansion | Product demos, tutorials, bug reports, changelogs |
| Week 4 | 1.2 buildup | Presenter Camera Bubble, UI polish, human demo angle |
| Final 2 days | Conversion push | App Store download, feedback, testimonials, Product Hunt prep |

## Reusable Post Angles

### Before / After

- Raw screen recording vs. MouseLens export.
- No zoom vs. smart zoom.
- Bare screen vs. styled background.
- Long recording vs. trimmed shareable clip.

### Pain Points

- "I recorded the right thing, but the viewer still cannot follow it."
- "A simple demo should not require a complex editing timeline."
- "Bug report videos often miss the exact interaction."
- "Tutorials lose people when the cursor disappears into the UI."

### Product Building Notes

- Why MouseLens records pointer events separately.
- Why the product stays focused instead of becoming a full editor.
- Why MP4 and GIF both matter.
- Why Presenter Camera Bubble belongs in 1.2 instead of earlier.

## 30-Day Production Rules

- Every week should include at least 3 real product visuals.
- At least 2 posts per week should include a GIF or video.
- Avoid promising 1.2 features as available until they ship.
- Use the same core phrase consistently: "Mac screen recording for polished product demos."
- End most posts with a simple question or feedback request, not a hard sell.

## Asset Checklist

- One 20-second product demo clip.
