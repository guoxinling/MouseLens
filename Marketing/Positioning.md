# MouseLens Positioning

Updated: 2026-07-14

## Current Context

- MouseLens 1.1 has passed review and is live.
- MouseLens 1.2 development is focused on Presenter Camera Bubble, UI polish, and later wallpaper/background system improvements.
- This document is for Marketing / Growth work only. It should not drive core-code changes directly.

## One-Line Positioning

MouseLens is a Mac screen recording tool that turns ordinary product walkthroughs into polished demo videos with smart zoom, cursor emphasis, beautiful backgrounds, and fast MP4/GIF export.

## Short Pitch

MouseLens helps makers, product teams, and educators create clear screen demos without opening a full video editor. Record your screen or a window, let MouseLens emphasize cursor movement and important interactions, then refine zoom, trim, background, and export settings before sharing.

## Core Promise

Record once. Get a demo that already looks edited.

## Primary Audience

### 1. Indie Developers and SaaS Builders

They need to ship feature demos, changelog videos, onboarding clips, and support walkthroughs quickly. They care about speed, clarity, and a polished product feel.

### 2. Product Managers and Growth Teams

They need short, repeatable demo clips for launches, internal updates, sales enablement, tutorials, and bug reproduction. They care about consistency and reducing the cost of making good-looking recordings.

### 3. Educators, Tutorial Creators, and Support Teams

They need screen recordings that make actions easy to follow. They care about visible cursor motion, click feedback, zoomed focus areas, and simple export.

## Use Cases

- Product demo for a new feature.
- App Store, website, or landing-page walkthrough clip.
- Tutorial for onboarding or help docs.
- Bug report video with clearer cursor movement and focus.
- Social media clip showing a product workflow.
- Short GIF for changelog, issue tracker, or community posts.

## Differentiation

### Compared With Basic Screen Recorders

Basic screen recorders capture what happened. MouseLens helps shape the recording into a clearer demo with smart zoom, cursor emphasis, backgrounds, trimming, and export controls.

### Compared With Full Video Editors

Full editors are flexible but slow for simple product walkthroughs. MouseLens is intentionally narrower: it focuses on the repeated workflow of recording an interface and making it presentable quickly.

### Compared With ScreenStudio-Style Tools

MouseLens should communicate a lightweight, local-first, Mac-native workflow. It should avoid sounding like a clone. The emphasis is: fast demo production, cursor-first clarity, clean defaults, MP4/GIF output, and a roadmap toward presenter camera and stronger background styling.

## Messaging Pillars

### 1. Smart Focus

MouseLens helps viewers follow the action with zoom moments, cursor reconstruction, and click emphasis.

Example copy:

- "Make every click easier to follow."
- "Guide attention automatically with smart zoom."
- "Turn cursor movement into a visual guide, not visual noise."

### 2. Polished Defaults

MouseLens makes recordings look more finished with backgrounds, padding, rounded corners, and clean export presets.

Example copy:

- "Your raw screen recording does not have to look raw."
- "Add a clean background and export a polished walkthrough."
- "Make demos feel ready to share without a full editing timeline."

### 3. Fast Demo Workflow

MouseLens is for the common demo loop: record, review, trim, adjust, export.

Example copy:

- "From recording to shareable demo in minutes."
- "Built for product walkthroughs, tutorials, and bug reports."
- "A focused workflow for the screen videos teams make every week."

### 4. Local-First Mac Utility

MouseLens should be positioned as a desktop utility that keeps recordings local unless the user chooses to share them elsewhere.

Example copy:

- "Record and edit locally on your Mac."
- "No account required for the core workflow."
- "A focused Mac app for private product recordings."
